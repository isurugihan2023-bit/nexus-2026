Advanced AI Features
Powerful AI tools to enhance your server experience.
!ai
Shows the AI Assistance Help Menu.
!tldr <number>
AI reads the last X messages in chat and gives you a summary of what you missed!
!roast @user
AI generates a hilarious gaming-style roast of the user.
!rizz @user
AI generates a smooth/funny pickup line.
!translate <text>
Translates any language into natural-sounding English.
!explain <topic>
Explains complex topics, code, or math step-by-step.
!idea <topic>
Brainstorms highly creative ideas for videos, stories, or games.
!name <theme>
Generates badass gaming tags or clan names.
!debate <topic>
Start an argument! The AI will fiercely argue the opposite side of your topic.
!imagine <prompt>
Generates AI images directly in Discord!

!nexusbrain
