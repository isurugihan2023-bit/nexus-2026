Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026.

What we store

  • Game sessions: display name, avatar, game name and activity details, start and last-seen times. Old history is deleted after about 30 days.
  • Voice activity: voice channel join/leave/move times used to measure time spent in voice.
  • Spotify now-playing: track title, artist, album, cover art URL, track link, and start/end times for members currently listening.
  • Game artwork URLs: public cover-art links per game (no user data).
  • Opt-out lists: user IDs that asked not to be shown.

What we show publicly

The public website shows display names, avatars, current games, voice presence, and Spotify now-playing. We never show passwords or login tokens, and the public pages do not show raw Discord user IDs — but display names and avatars are public while you are active.

How to opt out

  • Spotify display: send !spotify off in Discord to hide your listening activity (!spotify on to re-enable).
  • Game tracking display: to opt out of the game tracking display, contact us on our Discord server and we will remove it.

Deletion requests

To request deletion of your stored gameplay, voice, or Spotify display data, contact us via the Discord server. We will confirm what was removed.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Cover art and metadata may be fetched from third parties (e.g. game databases, Discord/Spotify CDNs) to display the site. Discord invite counts may be read from Discord's public invite API.
